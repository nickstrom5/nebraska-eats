What counts as an honor here

One source, reported as facts rather than scores: the James Beard Foundation. Its 2026 America's Classics award went to Johnny's Cafe in Omaha. David Utterback of Yoshitomo and Ota was a Best Chef: Midwest nominee in 2025 and 2026, Zach Midgett of Casa Bovina in Lincoln (2026) and Tim Nicholson of The Boiler Room in Omaha (2025) were semifinalists, and Le Bouillon in Omaha was a 2026 semifinalist for its wine program. Sehnert's Bakery in McCook, a 2019 America's Classic, closed in October 2025. Where an honor went to a chef, the line under the place names them.

James Beard Foundation and James Beard Award are trademarks of the James Beard Foundation. Nebraska Eats is not affiliated with it.
