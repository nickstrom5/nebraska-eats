Reubens & frenchees

7 places for two Nebraska sandwiches: the Reuben, which Omaha claims, and the breaded, fried cheese frenchee.

Ask for early accessThe free Nebraska Eats app for iPhone and iPad is on its way to the App Store.

A claim, not a certainty

Omaha says the Reuben was first made at the Blackstone Hotel; New York claims it too, so we call it a claim. Visit Omaha's 2025 Reuben roundup sends you to Crescent Moon, which it says proudly serves the original Blackstone Reuben, and to pubs and taverns across the city. Lincoln's visitor bureau lists the cheese frenchee at Don & Millie's.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Omaha (5), Lincoln (2).

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (a dated story such as the Flatwater Free Press's steakhouse series, or a visitor bureau's own page or listing for it) and its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score.
