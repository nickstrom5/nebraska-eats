Steak Town, reviewed

In 2025 the Flatwater Free Press sent its critic to Omaha's steakhouses and a few beyond. The December ranking put Committee Chophouse first, with The Drover and 801 Chophouse next; Johnny's Cafe, open since 1922, has the onion rings the critic calls Omaha's best. Outside Omaha the series found Ruhlman's in Ashland, Chances R in York and The Speakeasy, down a county road near Holdrege.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Omaha (9), York (1), Ashland (1), Holdrege (1).

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (a dated story such as the Flatwater Free Press's steakhouse series, or a visitor bureau's own page or listing for it) and its street address matched. No current source, or a website gone dark, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score.

Omaha Metro

10 steakhouses, by town.

  1. Ruhlman's Steakhouse

    603 US-6, Ashland

    Steakhouse

    Ashland's steakhouse, known for its wood-fired toffee skillet cookie (Flatwater Free Press, December 2025).

  2. 801 Chophouse

    1403 Farnam St, Omaha

    Steakhouse Douglas County Health Department inspection: Rated A, September 21, 2026

    The downtown chophouse dry-ages its beef for at least 28 days (Flatwater Free Press, December 2025).

  3. Brother Sebastian's Steakhouse

    1350 S 119th St, Omaha

    Steakhouse Douglas County Health Department inspection: Rated B, June 9, 2026

    Open since 1977 and reopened eight months after a 1996 fire; the St. Thomas filet is its steak (Flatwater Free Press, December 2025).

  4. Cascio's Steakhouse

    1620 S 10th St, Omaha

    Steakhouse Since 1946

    Old-school Italian recipes and a housemade spaghetti sauce, at its location since the 1940s (Flatwater Free Press).

  5. Gorat's

    4917 Center St, Omaha

    Steakhouse Since 1944 · Douglas County Health Department inspection: Rated A, September 23, 2026

    Open since 1944 on Center Street; its meat sauce follows a 1940s Gorat family recipe (Flatwater Free Press, December 2025).

  6. Jerico's

    11732 W Dodge Rd, Omaha

    Steakhouse

    Prime rib and a banana cream pie made in-house daily (Flatwater Free Press, December 2025); open since 1978.

  7. Johnny's Cafe

    4702 S 27th St, Omaha

    America's ClassicSteakhouse Since 1922

    The Stockyards steakhouse Frank Kawa opened in 1922, still run by his family; the Flatwater Free Press's critic calls its onion rings Omaha's best (December 2025).

  8. Pivot Prime

    13665 California St, Omaha

    Steakhouse Douglas County Health Department inspection: Rated B, July 28, 2026

    A new steakhouse near 132nd and West Dodge, known for its beef tallow candle (Flatwater Free Press, December 2025).

  9. The Committee Chophouse

    302 S 36th St, Omaha

    Steakhouse

    Ranked Omaha's best steakhouse by the Flatwater Free Press's critic (December 2025), in the renovated Cottonwood Hotel in Blackstone.

  10. The Drover

    2121 S 73rd St, Omaha

    Steakhouse Douglas County Health Department inspection: Rated A, August 27, 2026

    The "most beloved Omaha steakhouse" of the Flatwater Free Press's critic (December 2025); it opened in 1968 as Cork and Cleaver and became The Drover in 1979.

Lincoln & Southeast

1 steakhouse, by town.

  1. Chances R

    124 W 5th St, York

    Steakhouse Since 1932

    York's "pillar known statewide and built on beef and hospitality," a classic Interstate 80 stop (Flatwater Free Press, July 2025).

Central

  1. The Speakeasy

    72993 S Rd, Holdrege

    Steakhouse
